How it works

New here? The setup is simple.

Most people finish in a few minutes. No technical background needed.

1

Install on both phones

Install Vedetor on the guardian's phone and the phone to be monitored from Google Play, Cafe Bazaar, or direct download.

2

Choose a role

On your own phone choose Guardian. On the other phone choose Monitored device and review the consent notice.

3

Grant permissions

Location and SMS access are requested so live location, offline commands and SOS can work correctly.

4

Pair and go live

Copy the device ID, pair the two devices, and the dashboard becomes active with live updates.

FAQ

Questions people usually ask first

A clear page answers objections before they become support messages.

Does Vedetor work without internet?
Yes. When data is unavailable, SMS keeps the core communication flow alive so location and commands can still move.
Is Vedetor hidden on the device?
No. The monitored phone should know the app is active. Transparency is part of the product.
Can it be used for business devices too?
Yes. The same product can cover family safety and managed device fleets for companies or teams.
Which languages are available?
English, Persian and Arabic are supported in the current site.
Can I host it locally?
For deployments that need it, a domestic server option is part of the message and can be highlighted in onboarding.
